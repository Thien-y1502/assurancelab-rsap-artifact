# RSAP v2 claim ledger

## Supported

- The declared RSAP policy preserves the bounded false-PASS target in the
  frozen simulation and nine-point sensitivity grid.
- RSAP reduces median decision cost versus uniform e-process with a negative
  paired across-seed confidence interval.
- Safe coverage is not lower than the comparator in the aggregate; all traces
  are complete and budgets are respected.
- The finite implementation/probability audit passes for the declared
  predictable-factor certificate.

## Not supported

- No general optional-stopping theorem for arbitrary adaptive policies.
- No claim of superiority over every external scheduler.
- No enterprise cost, downtime or deployment claim; simulation-only by
  contract.
- No Q1 novelty claim before method-level literature audit and manuscript
  review.
