# RSAP v2 reviewer red-team

- Matched uniform streams reduce variance but do not replace an external
  workload; the loss model is still synthetic.
- The adaptive proof audit is finite and checks the implemented fixed-lambda
  factors; it does not prove every predictable policy theorem.
- Sensitivity shifts are bounded to the declared grid; larger shifts or
  adversarial utility poisoning remain open.
- The strongest fair baseline is uniform/risk-score e-process; naive policies
  are contextual, not the primary superiority comparator.
- Enterprise scheduling and real downtime economics are explicitly out of
  scope.
