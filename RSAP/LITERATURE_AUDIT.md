# RSAP literature boundary

Closest work includes the 2026 DRQN-CMDP adaptive audit scheduler, the recent
selective-monitoring/process-mining survey, Bayesian post-audits of security
investments, and auditor information-foraging studies. Those works motivate
budgeted selection or decision support. RSAP is restricted to an auditable
evidence-acquisition policy whose stopping risk remains controlled by an
anytime certificate; it is not presented as generic reinforcement learning.

Primary links: [DRQN-CMDP adaptive audit scheduling](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17305), [selective-monitoring survey](https://www.mdpi.com/2504-4990/8/8/234), [Bayesian post-audit](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jaccpubpol.2018.10.005), [auditor information foraging](https://phds.io/papers/01KXD9G250H0TT2GEH4H2QSCEC).
